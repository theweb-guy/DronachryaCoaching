About Us

Dronacharya Coaching Center

Dronacharya Coaching Center has earned a stellar reputation in Kabisuryanagar for its exceptional coaching tailored for CBSE students. Our primary goal is to foster an environment where students flourish academically and gain the confidence they need to succeed.

Established in 2019 by a group of passionate educators, our institution aims to equip learners with the tools and knowledge necessary for their academic goals. We take pride in our innovative teaching methodologies and individualized attention, making education a transformative experience.

Every year, we witness countless success stories from our students who have pursued their dreams in prestigious institutions. We are dedicated to continual improvement and excellence in our educational practices.
